# Course overview scripts for a presenter video

Each script is about 35 seconds when read at a natural pace (80 to 95 words). The host is introduced as CloudTech. They use only facts that are true of the courses today: no job or income promises, no invented numbers.

Use them three ways: read them to camera yourself, paste them into an AI avatar tool (HeyGen, D-ID, Hedra or similar) with your photo, or give them to the video builder for a voice-over.

If a video uses an AI avatar or a synthetic voice, say so in the description. TikTok, YouTube and ad platforms may also ask you to switch on their AI label.
