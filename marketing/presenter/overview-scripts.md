# Course overview scripts: read these on camera

Each script runs about 3 minutes at a relaxed pace (350 to 450 words). They are written to be **spoken**: short sentences, plain words, and a rhythm that is easy to read from a screen without sounding like you are reading.

## How to read them

- **Slow down.** Aim for about 140 words a minute. When in doubt, go slower.
- **(pause)** means stop for a full breath. Let it sit. It makes you sound sure.
- **[smile]**, **[look up]**, **[lean in]** are cues for you. They are not spoken.
- **[ON SCREEN: ...]** is a suggested caption or visual for the editor to add. You do not say it.
- Put the script on your laptop behind your camera, or use a free teleprompter app on your phone. Look at the lens when you can, especially on the first and last lines.
- Record in one take per section. If you stumble, pause, and say the line again. We can cut it.
- Stand or sit up straight, with good light on your face and a quiet room. The sound matters more than the picture.

## Rules these scripts follow

- Every fact is true of the course today. Nothing promises a job, a salary or a result.
- Nothing needs to be changed before you read it. If you want to say your name, add "I'm ..." after the first line.
- If you use an AI voice or an avatar for any part, say so in the description. The platforms may ask you to switch on their AI label.
