## POST 6: Academy · 🎙️📱 Screen recording
- **Pillar:** Tips (Excel)
- **Goal:** Value, saves
- **Hook:** "Stop using VLOOKUP like it's 2012."
- **Script:** XLOOKUP looks in any direction, doesn't break when you insert a column, and lets you say what to show if nothing is found. Type equals XLOOKUP, what you're looking for, where to look, what to return. Done. If your Excel is older and doesn't have it, you'll still see it in new workplaces, so learn it.
- **Visual plan:** Excel recording with a small product-price table. Type the formula slowly, zoom on each argument with a gold label.

- **Goal:** Simple explainer

- **Pillar:** Professional skills (Import & Export)
- **Goal:** Introduce professional courses with value first

- **Script:** Landed cost. Not the price on the supplier's invoice. The price plus shipping, customs duty, clearing charges and transport to your shop.
